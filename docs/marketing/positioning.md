# Mangasm — Brand Positioning

> ⚠️ **Shipping-truth guardrail.** Public-facing and App Store copy may claim ONLY features
> verified to ship (see `docs/appstore-4.3-remediation.md`). Claims that are roadmap-only —
> invitation/vouch-gated access, GPS privacy zones, end-to-end encryption, and astrology/
> numerology/zodiac AI matching — are quarantined in the **Roadmap** section below and must
> NOT appear in the listing, screenshots, or marketing until the build backs them. Telling a
> reviewer (or a user) a feature exists when it doesn't is misrepresentation, and worse than
> the 4.3 rejection this repositioning is meant to clear.

The gay events-and-community app where your reputation opens the door.

## Elevator Pitch

Mangasm is a safety-first events and community app for gay men — built around real-world
gatherings, not an endless swipe grid. Host and join member-run micro-events (Circles, Open
Doors, Social Mixers) where the exact address stays private until the host approves you. Build a
reputation in the community and unlock more as you become a trusted member. This is not swipe
culture. This is brotherhood at a higher frequency.

_(Shipping features only: member-hosted events with a defined taxonomy, host-approval address
gating, reputation-gated profile photos, 18+ gate + terms at first launch, block/report, full
account deletion.)_

## Target Personas

### 1. The Curator (25–38, urban professional)

Travels frequently. Dubai, London, Tokyo. Has been on every app. Deleted most of them. Values taste, discretion, and genuine connection. Quietly influential in his social circle. Will not tolerate algorithmic spam or unsolicited aggression. He wants a space that reflects who he actually is — not a storefront.

**What he needs from Mangasm:** Reputation-gated access that filters out bad actors. Member-hosted events with host-approval before an address is shared. A community that isn't a swipe storefront.

### 2. The Community Anchor (28–45, scene-adjacent)

Throws events. Hosts gatherings. Knows everyone worth knowing in his city. Is not on apps to date — he is on apps to find his people. Deeply loyal to the communities he trusts. Skeptical of corporate platforms that treat gay men as inventory.

**What he needs from Mangasm:** Host tools with approval-gated event access. Communities to build. A reputation system that rewards good behavior. Premium features that actually mean something.

### 3. The Newcomer (21–29, smaller city or newly out)

Smart, curious, and understated. Has felt unsafe or unwelcome on mainstream apps. Wants to meet men who share his values before he shares anything else. Drawn to the aesthetic — the luxury, the mystery, the sense that this is a room worth getting into.

**What he needs from Mangasm:** Reputation-gated photos that put trust before exposure. Host approval before an address is revealed. A community that treats consent and safety as default.

## Key Messages

1. **Safety is the product.** Reputation, host approval, block/report, 18+ gate — infrastructure, not a feature list.
2. **Reputation opens doors.** Access to photos and event addresses is earned through community standing, not universal.
3. **Meet through events, not a grid.** Member-hosted Circles, Open Doors, and Social Mixers — real-world first.
4. **Hosts control the door.** An event's exact address stays private until the host approves each attendee.
5. **This is a community, not a marketplace.** Events, communities, reputation — built for men who invest in their circles.

## Roadmap (NOT yet shipping — keep OUT of public / App Store copy until built)

These are genuine product intentions but are **not** in the current build. Do not claim them anywhere
public-facing until the code backs them and this note is removed:

- **Invitation / vouch-gated access** — today signup is standard email + Sign in with Apple; there is no invite or vouch gate.
- **GPS privacy zones** — currently cosmetic badges only; real coordinate fuzzing is not implemented.
- **End-to-end encrypted messaging** — not implemented; do not claim E2E encryption.
- **AI compatibility across astrology / numerology / zodiac** — unverified in the build; exclude until confirmed.

## Brand Voice and Tone

**What it sounds like:** Cormorant Garamond at 700 weight. Confident, unhurried. Never desperate. Never loud. Speaks from inside a room you want to be in. A maison, not a mall.

**Adjectives:** Dark. Luxurious. Quiet confidence. Knowing. Brotherhood. Selective. Warm inside, cool at the door.

**Sentence structure:** Short declaratives and fragments are fine. Restraint over explanation. Trust the reader.

**Never:** Exclamation marks used cheaply. Slang that dates. Explicit or coarse framing. Corporate-speak ("synergy", "ecosystem", "leverage"). Sycophancy ("the best", "revolutionary", "game-changing").

## Tagline Options

1. **"Enter the community."** — Imperative. Warm. The door, not the ad. Backed by what ships.
2. **"Reputation opens the door."** — States the actual mechanic (reputation + host approval).
3. **"Dark luxury. Brotherhood. Nightlife."** — The three-word brand stamp. Literal, rhythmic.
4. **"Not for everyone. On purpose."** — Declares selectivity without claiming an invite gate that isn't built.
5. **"Meet through events."** — Plain, differentiating, reviewer-safe.

**Lead with:** _Enter the community._ (Retire _"Where the signal shouldn't reach"_ until GPS privacy zones actually ship — it implies a privacy feature the build doesn't have.)

## Do / Don't List

### Do

- Frame 18+ content maturely: consent-first, trust-first, community-first.
- Use city names as aspirational shorthand (Dubai, London, Mykonos, Tokyo).
- Reference reputation, host approval, and brotherhood as the value system.
- Let the aesthetic speak: obsidian, gold, runway, Cormorant Garamond.
- Show the app in context: events, host approval, reputation — never explicit profiles.
- Use selectivity and reputation as a feature, not a flex.
- Credit the community as the product's backbone.

### Don't

- Use sexually explicit copy, imagery, or framing anywhere public-facing.
- Reference hookup culture, cruising, or App Store-unsafe terminology.
- Mock or diminish other platforms — be above it.
- Claim privacy zones, E2E encryption, invite-gating, or astrology AI until they ship (see Roadmap).
- Use rainbow-flag marketing as shorthand — this brand is more specific than that.
- Promise features that are not yet live (real backend, payments) in public copy.
- Use cheap urgency ("Limited time!", "Act now!").
