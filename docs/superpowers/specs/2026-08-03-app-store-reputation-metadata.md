# App Store / Play metadata — Community Reputation

## App Review notes (paste)

Community Reputation is a trust score. It increases when members complete verification, receive positive feedback, finish safety education, and help keep the community safe (including helpful reports). Higher scores unlock **cosmetic profile styles only** (backgrounds and accent colors). Reputation does **not** unlock messaging, adult content, visibility of private fields, or any sexual features. Users may freely switch among any styles they have unlocked.

## What’s New (template)

• Community Reputation cosmetic styles  
• Settings picker for unlocked looks  
• Clear trust-and-safety framing — no gamified adult metrics

## Privacy nutrition (related)

Reputation score is account data; preferred style is a user preference. Not used for advertising.
