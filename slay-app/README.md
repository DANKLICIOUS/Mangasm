# SLAY.LLC — The Grove | Web3 Meme Creation & Cryptocurrency Market Ecosystem

> **"A beautiful world that happens to contain a market."**

SLAY.LLC transforms cultural meme creation into continuous, liquid cryptocurrency markets. Built on the core product primitive of the **ARTIFACT**—a community-created meme identity with programmatic token liquidity.

---

## Architecture & Product Modules

1. **Ganesh Cinematic Hero Portal**
   - Immersive opening with canvas particle generation, sacred geometry, and progressive GNOMIE inhabitant reveals.
   - Minimalist, restrained luxury typography with ambient audio synthesis.

2. **The Grove (Discovery Layer)**
   - Live trending meme markets, new spores, and continuous bonding progress.
   - Real-time community activity streams and multidimensional health meters.

3. **GROW (Artifact Creation Protocol)**
   - Progressive disclosure workflow:
     1. Seed Conception & Ticker
     2. Meme Lore, Aura Color & Social Roots
     3. Transparent Creator Economics (1.0% Creator Share, 0.3% Platform, 99% Locked Liquidity)
     4. Mandatory Market Risk Disclosures & Ignition.

4. **Artifact Detail & Continuous Market Interface**
   - Dual view modes: **Beginner** ("What is happening?") vs. **Advanced** ("Show me the on-chain data").
   - Interactive SVG/Canvas price & volume chart with quadratic bezier interpolation.
   - 25SHA lifecycle audit trail and MonetizeOS commercial licensing metadata.

5. **GNOMIE Vision**
   - Intelligent informational market interpretation layer.
   - Multidimensional signal diagnostics: Transaction Velocity, Liquidity Depth, Volatility Index, Holder Distribution.

6. **GNOMIE School**
   - Zero-risk interactive learning environment.
   - Built-in mathematical simulators for bonding curve progression and slippage/price impact calculations.

7. **GNOMIE Identity & Reputation**
   - Four progression tiers: Novice → Expert → Mentor → Top 1%.
   - Reputation derived from transparent constructive participation signals (learning mastery, mentorship, artifact curation), not financial returns.

8. **Non-Custodial Wallet & Trade Engine**
   - Full 8-state connection machine (Disconnected, Connecting, Connected, Signature Required, Confirming, Confirmed, Failed).
   - Real-time trade simulations with transparent fee breakdowns (Creator reward, platform maintenance, network gas).

---

## Technical Stack
- **Framework**: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom SLAY design tokens (Obsidian Black `#050507`, Chrome `#F5F5F7`, Electric Green `#00FF66`, Turquoise `#06B6D4`, Iridescent Gradients)
- **Audio Engine**: Custom Web Audio API synthesizer for chimes, crystal ticks, bass swells, and harmonic chords.
- **Testing**: Vitest unit test suite covering domain integrity, economics, and learning curriculum.
